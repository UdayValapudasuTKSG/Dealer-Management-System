# Reference R5 — Reports, Dashboards & BI

All reporting, dashboard, and BI surfaces in AURA are computed **server-side** (corrected from client-side aggregation). Every query is **dealer-scoped** by `activeDealerId(res)` resolved from `x-dealer-id` — no report, chart, prediction, or export may read cross-dealer rows. Money is stored USD-scale and rendered GYD via `dealer.usdExchangeRate` (~209) at serialization; dates are bucketed in `America/Guyana` (GMT-4). Division splits are always `CAM` vs `GT`. Reports respect `role_permissions` (module×category) and `role_field_permissions` (`hidden|view|edit`): a field the caller cannot `view` is elided from report rows and from exports before the response leaves the server.

## R5.1 — The 10 Report Types (GET/POST /api/reports, per-type RBAC)

`/api/reports` exposes ten typed reports. Each takes a `type` discriminator plus a shared filter envelope; each has its own RBAC gate keyed on `role_permissions` for the reports module. Aggregation runs in Postgres/Drizzle (SUM/COUNT/window functions), never in the browser.

| # | Report type | Purpose | Persona visibility (RBAC) | Primary source tables | Key computed fields |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `sales_pipeline` | Open leads/deals by phase & stage with aging and conversion. | Advisor (own `ownerUserId`/`assignedTo`), Manager (division), Leadership (both divisions). | `leads`, `deals`, `stage_checklists` | Count & value by `phase`/`status`/`stage`, days-in-stage from `stageEnteredAt`, weighted value, stage-to-stage conversion %. |
| 2 | `sales_performance` | Advisor/division sales output vs. targets. | Advisor (self), Manager (team), Leadership (all). | `deals`, `dealer_users`, `invoices`, `payments` | Units by `salesAdvisorUserId`, gross from `otdPrice`, avg `discount`, avg `monthlyPayment`, close rate, avg deal cycle. |
| 3 | `inventory_aging` | Stock on hand, days-on-lot, hold exposure. | Manager, Leadership; Advisor read-only summary. | `vehicles`, `bookings`, `deals` | Count/value by `status` (`available\|reserved\|booked\|delivered\|in_transit\|sold\|service`), days since intake, aging buckets (0-30/31-60/61-90/90+), `holdUntil`/`holdReason` exposure. |
| 4 | `finance_applications` | Funding funnel & approval throughput. | Manager, Leadership, Finance role. | `finance_applications`, `banks`, `los_submissions` | Count/amount by `status` (`pending→submitted→under_review→approved\|declined→disbursed`), approval rate, avg `apr` vs `bank.baseApr`, avg `termMonths`, LOS latency (`losConnector`/`mode`), decline reasons from `statusHistory[]`. |
| 5 | `service_workshop` | Service order throughput, bay/technician load, WIP. | Service Manager, Leadership; Advisor excluded. | `service_orders`, `job_cards`, `service_invoices` | Orders by `status`, avg turnaround from `scheduledDate`, `laborHours`×`laborRate`, tech utilization by `technician`, job-card `status` mix (`open→in_progress→completed`). |
| 6 | `parts_inventory` | Parts stock, reorder exposure, consumption. | Parts/Service Manager, Leadership. | `parts`, `job_card_parts`, `part_purchases`, `suppliers` | `stock` vs `reorderLevel` (below-reorder flag), stock value at `cost`, issue/return movement, supplier lead time from `part_purchases`. |
| 7 | `revenue_receivables` | Billed vs. collected, outstanding AR. | Finance, Manager, Leadership. | `invoices`, `payments`, `receipts`, `service_invoices` | Issued/paid/void by `invoices.status` (`issued\|partially_paid\|paid\|void`), collections by `payments.method` (`cash\|card\|bank_transfer\|cheque\|mobile_money\|financing`), outstanding balance, aging of receivables. Internal ledger only (no gateway). |
| 8 | `tax_gra` | GRA (Guyana Revenue Authority) tax basis & filing register. | Finance, Leadership; gated by `gra_filing` approvals. | `dealer_taxes`, `invoices`, `quotes`, `gates` | **Server-computed** tax per `dealer_taxes` (`kind VAT\|duty\|excise\|levy`, VAT 0%/14%, `threshold`, `excludeEv` import-duty exemption); taxable base, VAT collected, duty; filing status from `gates` of `type=gra_filing`. AI never invents TIN/CIF/duty — extraction is legible-fields-only, tax is deterministic. |
| 9 | `delivery_operations` | Delivery/handover throughput & PDI/CSAT quality. | Manager, Leadership, Delivery advisors. | `deliveries`, `assets`, `coverage_plans` | Deliveries by step of the 9-step flow (`sales_order→pdi_checklist→registration→insurance→invoice→appointment→delivery→signature→feedback`), avg cycle to `deliveredAt`, PDI pass rate from `pdiItems[]`, CSAT from `feedbackRating`/`feedbackComment`. |
| 10 | `agent_activity` | AI agent throughput, confidence, HITL & audit. | Manager, Leadership, Admin. | `agents`, `agent_runs`, `gates`, `timeline_events` | Runs by `agent.key`/`domain`, `tasksToday`, `successRate`, avg `confidence` vs `MIN_AGENT_CONFIDENCE`, below-threshold→HITL rate, avg `latency`, autonomous-write count (A1/A2/A11), review outcomes. |

- **Date range** — `from`/`to` bucketed in `America/Guyana`; presets today/WTD/MTD/QTD/YTD.
- **Division** — `divisionId` (`CAM`/`GT`/both); Advisors/Managers are pre-scoped to their `dealer_users.divisionId`.
- **Owner/advisor** — `ownerUserId`, `assignedTo`, `salesAdvisorUserId`, `technician`; Advisors forced to self.
- **Dimensional** — `make`/`model`/`year`/`powertrain`, `channel`/`source` (via `lead_sources`), `bankId`, `status`/`phase`/`stage` enums, `loyaltyTier`, `accountType person|business`.
- All filters translate to WHERE/GROUP BY in Drizzle; the response carries only aggregates and, where requested, a paginated drill-down of visible rows.

## R5.2 — Dashboard Endpoints (GET /api/dashboard/*, dealer-scoped, server-computed)

Corrected: these were previously assembled client-side; all six now aggregate in Postgres and return finished, view-permission-filtered payloads. Money is GYD-converted at serialization.

| Endpoint | Returns | Source tables / agents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/dashboard/summary` | Headline KPIs: open leads, active deals, MTD units & gross (`otdPrice`), available inventory count/value, outstanding AR, service WIP, today's tasks/appointments. Deltas vs prior period. | `leads`, `deals`, `vehicles`, `invoices`, `service_orders`, `tasks`. |
| `/dashboard/pipeline` | Funnel by `phase`/`status`/`stage` with count, value, weighted value, and stage aging from `stageEnteredAt`; conversion between adjacent stages. Same data backing the `sales_pipeline` report. | `leads`, `deals`, `stage_checklists`. |
| `/dashboard/sales-performance` | Per-advisor and per-division leaderboard: units, gross, avg `discount`, close rate, avg cycle; target attainment. Advisor sees self + anonymized rank; Manager sees team; Leadership sees both divisions. | `deals`, `dealer_users`, `invoices`. |
| `/dashboard/inventory-breakdown` | Stock composition by `status`, `make`/`model`/`year`/`powertrain`, division; aging buckets; hold exposure (`holdUntil`/`holdReason`); reserved/booked via `bookings`. | `vehicles`, `bookings`, `deals`. |
| `/dashboard/predictions` | AI-assisted forward view: projected units/gross, deal close-probability from `lead.aiScore`, at-risk deals, inventory sell-through ETA. Backed by A15 pipeline suggestions. Every generation obeys `isAgentEnabled` kill-switch, records `recordAgentRun`, PII-minimizes, and drops any output below `MIN_AGENT_CONFIDENCE` (surfaced as "insufficient confidence," not shown as fact). | `leads.aiScore`, `deals`, `vehicles`, `agent_runs`, A15. |
| `/dashboard/sentiment` | Customer sentiment digest across calls and messaging: distribution positive/neutral/negative, trend, flagged conversations. Sourced from A6 call sentiment (`call_logs.sentiment`) and the sentiment digest agent over WhatsApp/email. All inbound text is wrapped with `guardUntrusted` before any model call. | `call_logs`, `whatsapp_messages`, `email_logs`, `agent_runs`, A6. |

## R5.3 — Persona Dashboards

The same endpoints power three role-scoped landing dashboards; RBAC and `role_field_permissions` decide which tiles/fields render, and division scope is applied server-side.

| Persona | Scope | Composition (tiles & sources) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales Advisor** | Own leads/deals; own `divisionId`. | My open leads by stage + single **Advance** CTA, my active deals & MTD units, my tasks/appointments/test-drives, my A7 outreach drafts pending Approve&Send, my quotes (`current`/`superseded`). From `summary`+`pipeline`+`sales-performance`(self). |
| **Sales/Service Manager** | Team within one division (CAM or GT). | Team leaderboard, division pipeline & aging, inventory breakdown for division, finance funnel, open `gates` awaiting approval (below-floor, credit-decline, gra_filing…), agent activity. From all six endpoints, division-filtered. |
| **Leadership** | Both divisions, dealer-wide. | CAM vs GT side-by-side on summary/performance/inventory, consolidated AR & tax (GRA), predictions, sentiment digest, cross-division agent audit. Full `view` on all fields subject to role config. |

## R5.4 — Review Disambiguation on Dashboards

Corrected: dashboard tiles labeled "Review" are explicitly disambiguated so counts never conflate distinct queues.

- **Readiness Review** — `POST /leads/:id/review` / `/advance` 422 `{unmet:[]}` against versioned `stage_checklists`; the single Advance CTA. Not an approval.
- **Approvals Review** — the `gates` queue (`below_floor_price|credit_decline|capital_order|gra_filing|refund_release|stage_advance`) with `evidence[]`/`resolution`.
- **Approve-run Review** — HITL over AI output: `/documents/extraction/review` (A5), `/agents/runs/:id/review`, A7 Approve&Send, `/gra/extract` confirmation. Only sub-threshold or autonomous-write runs land here.

## R5.5 — Export (CSV / Excel / PDF)

- Every report and dashboard drill-down exports via a `format=csv|xlsx|pdf` parameter; the file is built **server-side** from the same dealer-scoped, RBAC-filtered aggregate — fields hidden by `role_field_permissions` never appear in the file.
- **CSV/XLSX** — flat aggregate + optional drill-down rows; money exported as GYD with the applied `usdExchangeRate` noted; dates in `America/Guyana`. Mirrors the `/vehicles/import` xlsx conventions.
- **PDF** — rendered with `pdfkit` (same engine as `quote-pdf.ts` and the delivery invoice), branded per `dealer`/division, with filter header and generation timestamp.
- Large exports are queued and delivered through the storage layer (`/storage/uploads/request-url`, presigned GCS URL via the Replit sidecar) with dealer-scoped object-storage ACL — no cross-dealer object is ever addressable.
- Every export is written to `audit-logs` (dealer-scoped) and `activity`.

## R5.6 — Natural-Language "Ask Your Data"

- The Concierge (`/api/anthropic/conversations` SSE + `/api/copilotkit`) answers NL analytics questions, but its grounding is **dealer-scoped** — it may only read the current dealer's aggregates (corrected).
- It does **not** free-form query the database: it maps intent to the existing `/dashboard/*` and `/reports` endpoints, so numbers stay server-computed and RBAC/field-permission filtered; CopilotKit tools remain nav-only.
- Powered by `claude-sonnet-4-6` under the standard guardrails: `isAgentEnabled` kill-switch, `recordAgentRun` audit, PII-minimization, `guardUntrusted` on any user/inbound text, and `MIN_AGENT_CONFIDENCE` (below-threshold answers defer rather than assert). It never fabricates figures — it cites the report/endpoint that produced them.
