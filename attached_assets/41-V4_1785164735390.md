# Validation V4 — Open Items & Honest Limits

**Scope statement (per honesty contract).** AURA is **foolproof within a bounded envelope**: the guarantee is *application-logic correctness plus the tenant-isolation, money-ledger, and AI-governance invariants* (see `#r-invariants`, `#r4`, `#r10`). Concretely, that envelope covers: canonical state machines (`#r2`) with server-enforced transition guards; tenant scoping on *every* data-plane path (including concierge, audit, storage) where a foreign row returns `404` and never `403` (no existence leak); the RBAC → entitlement pipeline (`INV-ENT-1`, un-entitled module/agent → `404` hidden, enforced server-side); idempotency semantics (`409` in-flight / `422 key_reuse_mismatch`); the immutable payment ledger with offsetting-entry reversal; stage gates (the canonical six of `#r4/NC-4`) and inline reviews; read-only, hard-blocked super-admin impersonation; and the SAGA discipline (durable per-step markers, per-step compensation, no DB transaction held across an external GCS/SMTP/LOS call).

**What "foolproof" does NOT mean.** The spec makes **no claim of production or NFR readiness**. Non-functional guarantees — disaster recovery / backup, encryption-at-rest, high-availability / uptime SLOs, distributed tracing, and a durable message broker — are **PENDING-INFRA** and are the **pre-GA blockers**. These are boundaries of the guarantee, not covered by it. **AURA is foolproof within these boundaries; these are the boundary.**

## V4.1 — r9 vs V4: what each list contains

| List | Meaning | Status semantics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `#r9` (Corrected-Design Deltas) | Defects observed in the *built* application **plus** the corrected-design mandate (e.g. first-class `test_drives`, `service_cases`, `reviews` per `NC-8`; "reserved/booked" vocabulary; 9-step delivery). | **to-fix** — known gap between built app and target design; each has a defined correction. |

## V4.4 — PENDING-INFRA (the pre-GA blockers, NFR — NOT claimed foolproof)

These are non-functional and explicitly outside the correctness guarantee. Each must be resolved before a production/GA claim can be made.

| # | NFR blocker | Impact if shipped without it | Who decides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N1 | **Disaster recovery / backup** (PostgreSQL PITR, tested restore, RPO/RTO targets) | Data-loss and prolonged-outage risk; no proven recovery. No RPO/RTO commitment. | Platform SRE + business (set RPO/RTO). |
| N2 | **Encryption-at-rest** (DB + GCS blobs + secrets/KMS) | Confidentiality exposure of PII, documents, and financial records if storage is compromised; compliance gap (ties to `#r10`). | Platform security. |
| N3 | **High-availability / uptime** (multi-instance Express, DB failover, uptime SLO) | Single-point-of-failure; no availability guarantee; maintenance = downtime. | Platform SRE + business (SLO). |
| N4 | **Distributed tracing / observability** (request tracing, metrics, structured audit correlation across the 163 paths + SAGA steps) | Incidents hard to diagnose; SAGA compensation and cross-tenant issues difficult to trace; weak operational assurance. | Platform SRE. |
| N5 | **Durable message broker** (async backbone for the `202` flows: enquiries, remind, export, outreach) | Async work (currently in-process/outbox `email_logs`) lacks durable queueing, retry, and back-pressure; at-least-once delivery not guaranteed under load; scaling ceiling. | Platform architecture. |

## V4.5 — Restatement of the boundary

- **In-envelope (guaranteed):** application-logic correctness; tenant isolation (`404`-hides-foreign, no `403` leak); money-ledger integrity (immutable entries, offsetting reversal); AI governance (three autonomous writers `A1/A6/A16` only, audited + kill-switchable; deterministic `A2/A3/A11` not AI-writes; HITL/advisory never auto-send; platform-reserved `governance` kill-switch per `NC-13`); canonical enums/state machines (`#r2`); idempotency; entitlement gate (`INV-ENT-1`); read-only impersonation with hard-blocked money/gate/customer-send; SAGA multi-step with compensation.
- **Out-of-envelope (open / deferred / PENDING-INFRA):** O1–O11 functional/integration items and N1–N5 NFR blockers above.
- **AURA is foolproof within these boundaries; these are the boundary.** A production/GA readiness claim requires closing all N1–N5 pre-GA blockers and the compliance-critical functional items (O1 gateway/reconciliation, O2 GRA e-filing, O3 DSR/retention infra) as decided by their named owners.
