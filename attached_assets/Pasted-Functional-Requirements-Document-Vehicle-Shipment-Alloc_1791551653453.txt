Functional Requirements Document: Vehicle Shipment Allocation & Lead Tracking Flow

Document Overview: This document outlines the functional requirements, workflow logic, state transitions, and automated communications for managing vehicle shipments, allocations, VIN assignments, and lead advancement within the Dealership Management System (DMS).
1. Executive Summary & Objectives
The objective of this feature implementation is to enhance the existing Dealership Management System (DMS) by introducing a structured workflow for tracking vehicle shipments and linking customer leads to physical/expected inventory. This flow ensures that a customer lead cannot transition to the "Vehicle Allocated" state without being validly assigned to an active shipment, provides automated email tracking for estimated arrival dates, and enables flexible re-assignment and automated VIN matching.

3. Key System Requirements & Capabilities
3.1 Shipment Creation & Management
The DMS must support two distinct mechanisms for creating and managing incoming vehicle shipments:
File Import Method: Support standard file upload (e.g., CSV/Excel) allowing bulk entry of shipment details, vehicle models, trim levels, colors, quantities, and expected delivery dates.
UI-Focused Interactive View:
An intuitive interface enabling users to create a new shipment entry.
Ability to build a shipment by selecting an assortment of vehicle models, variants, and exterior/interior colors.
Assignment of a target delivery timeline specified by month and year (e.g., November 2026).
3.2 Lead Lifecycle & Advancement Rules
Advancement Gate: Moving a customer from the "Pre-Book" status to "Vehicle Allocated" requires a hard validation check verifying that the lead has been assigned to a valid shipment containing matching vehicle specs.
Re-assignment Flexibility: Dealership staff must be allowed to edit or re-assign a customer's allocated shipment. This accommodates scenarios where customer preferences change (e.g., color/model change) or logistics schedules require shifting leads across different shipment batches.
3.3 Automated Customer Communications
The system must automatically trigger transactional notifications based on key workflow events:
Initial Allocation Email: Triggered immediately upon advancing to "Vehicle Allocated".
Outlines vehicle allocation details.
Displays the estimated shipment ETA date.
Required Legal/Policy Disclaimer: Must explicitly state that the ETA is an estimate and subject to change based on logistics and transit variables.
ETA Shift / Update Email: Triggered whenever dealership staff edits shipment arrival dates.
Identifies the revised estimated ETA date.
Contains clear wording informing the customer of the date shift.
Includes the required disclaimer highlighting that the updated ETA remains subject to change.
3.4 VIN Upload & Automated Matching Engine
When physical VIN numbers become available for a shipment, the system must execute an automated assignment process:
VIN File Upload: Interface to import VIN lists associated with a specific Shipment ID.
Randomized Auto-Matching Logic: The system must automatically and randomly match incoming VINs to allocated customers under the following strict rules:
Exact match on vehicle Model.
Exact match on vehicle Color and Trim specifications.
Random distribution among eligible customers to eliminate manual bias.
Audit & Reporting: Generate an assignment summary log showing successful VIN-to-customer mappings and alerting staff of any unassigned VINs or unmatched customers.
4. Summary of Functional Rules & Logic
Rule 1: No lead shall advance to "Vehicle Allocated" without an active shipment reference.
Rule 2: Any update to a shipment's target date must trigger a batch notification to all assigned customers.
Rule 3: Re-assignment of shipments must re-validate color and model suitability before confirmation.
Rule 4: Automated VIN assignment must respect variant and color parity prior to random selection.
