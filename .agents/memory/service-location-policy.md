---
name: Service appointment location
description: Dealer-owned service addresses are distinct from showroom locations.
---
Service appointments must support their own dealer-configured address for the WhatsApp agent.

**Why:** The user requested the same customization available for test drives, independently for service visits.

**How to apply:** Keep service and showroom settings separate and dealer-scoped. Preserve the showroom fallback when no service override is provided; do not infer workshop addresses from customer addresses or another dealer.
