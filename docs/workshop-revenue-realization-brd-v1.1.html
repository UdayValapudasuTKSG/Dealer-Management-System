Daily Workshop WIP & Completed-Uninvoiced Revenue Realization Report

Business Requirements Document · Version 1.1
Date: October 6, 2026 · Status: Business requirements approved; technical prerequisites remain open
Audience: Workshop Management, Service Management, Finance and Engineering

Approval boundary. This document revises the supplied v1.0 BRD following a source-code review. The user approved this requirements baseline and its recommended defaults. Approval is not evidence of implementation or production-data validation. Unresolved mappings and prerequisites still require confirmation. Branch authorization, sublet billing and GL reconciliation must not be presented as existing capabilities without verification.

1. Purpose and business outcomes

Provide service and finance managers with a daily, auditable view of workshop work not yet invoiced. Identify completed jobs requiring billing attention, incomplete pricing and operational delays without creating invoices, sending customer invoices or changing accounting balances.

  • Reduce the elapsed time between physical completion and invoice issuance.
  • Expose omitted chargeable labour, parts and other approved charges.
  • Distribute invoicing throughout the month rather than concentrating it at month-end.
  • Identify a responsible advisor and technician, or explicitly flag missing assignments.

Success measures: count and value of completed-uninvoiced jobs older than 24 hours; median completion-to-invoice time; number of unresolved pricing exceptions; successful scheduled delivery rate. Establish baselines before rollout and agree improvement targets with Finance. Estimated uninvoiced value is not recognized revenue, profit, cash due or proven revenue loss.

3. Scope and existing foundations

In scope: configurable scheduled delivery, scoped recipients, inline HTML summary, PDF and XLSX attachments, optional CSV export, authorized drill-down, persisted report snapshots, pricing/aging warnings, delivery audit and explicit empty-state reporting. Branch isolation and accounting reconciliation remain original requirements, subject to the prerequisites below.

Out of scope: automatic invoice creation or customer invoice dispatch, payment collection, automatic GL adjustments, automatic stock movements, automatic pricing corrections, and unrelated sales reporting.

AURA currently provides operational WIP reporting, workshop timestamps, service-pricing calculations, dealer-local dates, email queues, recipient-policy mechanisms and report export infrastructure. These should be reused where appropriate. Existing WIP is not the financial report specified here; an existing stock location does not establish staff branch access.

Alternative to this change: managers can inspect live WIP and billing records separately. That does not provide a single scheduled, financially consistent exception report.

4. Definitions and population rules

Reporting unit: one repair-order/job-card billing unit with an unambiguous parent service-order reference. Confirm any one-to-many relationships before implementing totals. Count each billing unit once; joins must not multiply labour, parts or RO counts.

Category Required inclusion rule
Received — not started Vehicle physically received, work not started, not cancelled and no issued service invoice. Show separately from commenced WIP.
Work in Progress Work has started, including on-hold work, but the job is not physically completed or cancelled and has no issued service invoice. Retain the actual workflow status and waiting reason.
Work Complete — uninvoiced Physically completed, not cancelled, and no issued service invoice as of the snapshot cutoff.
Closed without invoice — exception Closed jobs without an issued service invoice. Count once, show as an explicit exception rather than silently dropping them or treating them as normal WIP.
Excluded from uninvoiced totals Future/unreceived bookings, cancelled jobs, and jobs with issued service invoices. Invoiced-but-unpaid balances belong in receivables, not this report.

Draft estimates and quotations are not issued invoices. Voided/cancelled invoice treatment must be mapped to AURA's real document lifecycle. A credit note, financial adjustment or parts return must never automatically restore the whole job to the uninvoiced population. Orphaned charges or inconsistent statuses are data-quality exceptions.

Separate customer-pay jobs from warranty, internal, goodwill and other configured pay types. Report operational counts for all authorized types, but do not label non-customer-pay amounts as customer-collectible revenue. Finance must approve their valuation basis.

5. Valuation and calculation rules

  • Currency: GYD, with explicit currency labels. Reuse AURA's labour-rate resolution and conversion rules; do not introduce a second FX conversion.
  • Hours sold: customer-billable quoted labour hours, using AURA's existing legacy fallback where no override exists. Actual technician time is a separate operational measure, never an automatic replacement.
  • Labour value: use the canonical service-pricing calculation and applied rate. Do not independently multiply a potentially stale rate in the report.
  • Parts value: use the same unbilled chargeable lines as the canonical invoice/estimate calculation. Net returns at their applicable price and exclude lines already billed separately. Show allocated/reserved inventory separately from chargeable value; do not treat reservation as physical issue.
  • Quantity: countable part quantities and oil quantities in litres must have separate columns or units. Do not sum unlike units into a single parts count.
  • External parts and sublet: keep externally sourced parts distinct from outsourced service labour. Supplier cost and customer selling value are separate measures. Only customer-chargeable selling value contributes to the invoice estimate.
  • Other charges: include applicable shipping, duties, issued oil, approved surcharges and other items supported by canonical service pricing. Preserve component visibility to explain the total.
  • Discounts and approvals: reflect only amounts authorized by existing pricing rules. Pending discounts, duty/tax exemptions or other approvals are exceptions, not granted savings.
  • Taxes and rounding: use the same taxable basis, tax rules and rounding order as AURA service pricing. Sum the resulting line/component amounts, not a separately rounded report formula.
Estimated invoice value = canonical unbilled chargeable parts + billable labour + supported customer-chargeable extras − applicable approved discounts + applicable taxes.
Allocated-but-not-chargeable value and supplier costs are disclosed separately and excluded from this total. Missing prices are marked Not valued, not silently represented as a known zero. Aggregates containing missing values must be labelled incomplete.

The existing pricing service may perform initialization or other side effects. Reporting must use a read-only calculation path or pre-established configuration; generating a report must not write tax defaults, pricing decisions, invoices or inventory changes.

6. Required report content

6.1 Header and executive summary

Include dealership and branch scope, report ID, scheduled reporting date, actual cutoff timestamp, timezone, currency, generation time, valuation basis and completeness/reconciliation status.

Summary rows: Received—not started; WIP; Work Complete—uninvoiced; Closed-without-invoice exceptions; and total estimated uninvoiced value. Display RO count, billable hours, parts selling value, labour value, other selling charges, approved discounts, estimated tax and estimated invoice total. Provide pay-type breakdowns. Display allocated-only value separately.

An Urgent Action Required section identifies completed-uninvoiced jobs over 24 hours old. Unknown completion times appear in a separate data-quality section.

6.2 Detail table and exports

Each row shall contain:

  • RO/job-card number and parent reference; branch; reporting category and actual status.
  • Customer name, vehicle description, registration/VIN where available, and pay type.
  • RO creation date, received date, work-start date, completion date, workshop age and completed-unbilled hours.
  • Assigned service advisor and technician; show “Unassigned” rather than using the creator as a substitute.
  • Countable parts quantity, oil litres, unbilled parts selling value, allocated-only value, billable hours and labour selling value.
  • External parts, sublet supplier cost and selling value where supported, other charges, discounts, taxes and estimated total.
  • Action flags, waiting reason, recorded next action/follow-up date, valuation completeness, reconciliation state and authorized drill-down reference.

Sort each section by workshop age descending, then stable RO/job-card identifier. Sort urgent completed jobs by completion age descending. Missing fields remain visibly unknown; no inferred advisor, price, branch or date.

PDF and XLSX attachments are mandatory; CSV is an additional export, not a substitute for XLSX. All formats derive from the same saved snapshot and access-filtered payload. Long PDFs repeat column headings and must not truncate rows. Spreadsheet exports must neutralize formula-injection values from customer-supplied text.

7. Aging and action rules

ID Rule
BR-01 Workshop age uses dealership-local calendar days since physical receipt. If absent, use a known work-start timestamp labelled “Legacy fallback”. If both are absent, show “Unknown”; never use booking creation silently.
BR-02 Urgent means more than 24 elapsed hours between valid completion and cutoff. Exactly 24 hours is not greater than 24. The calculation is not based on calendar-day boundaries.
BR-03 After reopening, the job returns to its applicable operational category. A later completion starts a new completion-aging interval; preserve the prior transition history.
BR-04 Completed jobs with missing chargeable parts prices or expected billable labour hours receive “Pending Cost Allocation”. Zero parts, zero hours and no charges each receive distinct review flags.
BR-05 Legitimate labour-only, parts-only, warranty and no-charge work must be distinguishable from missing allocation. Any exception acknowledgement requires an authorized actor, reason and audit history.
BR-06 Warnings use explicit text/icons in addition to red styling so they remain meaningful in monochrome PDF, Excel and accessible views.

8. Scheduling and delivery

Proposed default: report disabled until configured; after opt-in, daily at 18:00 in the dealership's configured timezone, including weekends. A 07:00 next-business-morning option requires an approved working-day/holiday calendar and clear prior-day reporting semantics.

  • Administrators configure enabled state, local dispatch time, cadence, branch scope and eligible recipients. Record configuration changes and provide an authorized preview/test action.
  • Generation runs asynchronously and must not block workshop requests. Define load-test volume and acceptable delivery delay before release; do not promise exact-to-the-second delivery.
  • Persist one consistent snapshot per scheduled occurrence and scope. Record scheduled time and actual cutoff separately. If late, label it late; do not present a current-state calculation as historical as-of data.
  • Proposed recovery: generate one late report for the most recent missed eligible occurrence and audit older missed occurrences without flooding recipients. Business approval is required.
  • Use stable occurrence/snapshot and recipient identities for duplicate prevention. Retries reuse the saved report, not recalculated totals. Ambiguous provider outcomes require reconciliation rather than blind duplicate sending.
  • Use only the dealership's configured email sender. Never fall back to a different dealership or global sender. Apply current permissions again at dispatch.
  • Record queued, provider-accepted, failed and cancelled states honestly. Provider acceptance does not prove inbox delivery. Surface permanent failures to authorized administrators.
  • If the authorized population and exceptions are empty, send “No Uninvoiced Work Outstanding” with zero-count attachments. Missing branch assignments or unvalued records must not produce this clean empty state.

9. Access, privacy and branch prerequisites

Every read, saved snapshot, attachment and drill-down must be dealership-scoped. Branch-only delivery additionally requires explicit job-to-branch assignment and user-to-branch grants. Department/role routing is a selection mechanism, not a replacement for these authorization checks.

Unknown-branch records are visible only in an authorized multi-site exception report. Branch-restricted users must not receive them or other branches' aggregates. Missing branch infrastructure blocks branch reporting; a dealership-wide interim release requires explicit scope approval.

Respect financial field restrictions in HTML, PDF, XLSX/CSV and drill-down, including derived totals. A hidden component must not be recoverable by subtracting visible components from an unrestricted total. Proposed recipient policy: active internal AURA users with explicit report and relevant financial access; external recipients remain disabled unless a separately approved policy is implemented.

Require TLS/SSL or enforced STARTTLS with certificate verification for sending and HTTPS for downloads. Block non-encrypted sender configurations for this report. TLS is transport protection, not protection against forwarding. Keep exports private; do not expose customer information through public or long-lived unprotected links.

Retention duration and deletion policy require approval before launch. Log access and delivery metadata without duplicating full customer/report contents in application logs. Email attachments cannot be revoked after delivery; downloaded copies are outside application access control.

10. Accounting reconciliation

Mandatory AURA reconciliation: for the same snapshot, population and permissions, report component totals must reconcile exactly to the canonical service-pricing result, within its defined monetary precision. Any missing inputs must be visible.

Original GL requirement retained as a dependency: Finance must specify accounting system/company, accounts, recognition policy, cost versus selling-value basis, tax treatment, cutoffs, currency, mapping keys and tolerances. Unbilled retail estimates cannot be assumed equal to posted WIP cost or GL pending entries.

ERPNext synchronization is asynchronous. The report must distinguish Reconciled, Pending sync, Variance, Not applicable and Not configured, with an as-of timestamp. Do not label the report “100% GL reconciled” unless the mapped comparison has actually passed. Reporting must not post balancing entries or hide differences.

A release without GL reconciliation is a reduced-scope operational report and requires explicit approval; it does not satisfy the original GL acceptance requirement.

11. Acceptance criteria

ID Testable result
AC-01 Population A fixture containing future bookings, received-only, started, on-hold, completed, closed-without-invoice, cancelled and invoiced jobs puts each eligible unit in exactly one defined category. Paid/unpaid status does not turn issued invoices back into uninvoiced work.
AC-02 Billing boundaries Previously separately billed parts and net returns are not charged twice. Reservation-only value is separately disclosed. Quantity counts do not include oil litres.
AC-03 Labour Changing timer hours alone does not change sold hours or labour revenue. A valid quoted-hours change follows the same calculation and approval rules as service pricing.
AC-04 Valuation For fixed fixtures, report component totals match canonical service-pricing totals. A missing price yields an incomplete-value warning, not a misleading fully valued zero.
AC-05 Pay types Customer-pay, warranty, internal and goodwill cases remain separately identifiable; customer-collectible totals do not silently include non-customer-pay estimates.
AC-06 Warnings A completed job with an unpriced chargeable part is flagged. Labour-only and parts-only fixtures receive appropriate review treatment rather than an unexplained error. Approved exceptions remain auditable.
AC-07 Aging Completion ages of 23:59, 24:00 and 24:01 produce non-urgent, non-urgent and urgent results. Local-midnight and daylight-saving fixtures preserve elapsed-hour correctness and local workshop-day aging.
AC-08 Reopening/history Reopening removes the job from completed-uninvoiced urgency; recompletion uses the new completion event. Missing historical timestamps display Unknown rather than an invented age.
AC-09 Snapshot parity HTML, PDF and XLSX/CSV totals and rows match a single report ID. An invoice created after the cutoff does not alter that saved report but is reflected in the next report and live drill-down.
AC-10 Scheduling Configured dispatch follows dealership local time. Restart/retry does not create another logical delivery for the same occurrence and recipient. Late recovery is labelled and follows the approved policy.
AC-11 Empty state A genuinely empty authorized scope receives the required no-outstanding-work message and empty exports. Unassigned-branch or unvalued exceptions prevent a false clean result.
AC-12 Isolation Dealer A cannot view or receive Dealer B data. A branch-only user sees only assigned branches in every format; multi-site access requires an explicit grant. Unknown branches fail closed.
AC-13 Permission changes Removing a recipient or financial permission before dispatch prevents the previously queued unauthorized attachment from being sent. Restricted money cannot be inferred from totals or exports.
AC-14 Delivery safety Missing/disabled SMTP or non-encrypted transport blocks sending and produces an actionable status. No sender fallback occurs. Failed delivery is never reported as delivered.
AC-15 GL states Matched, pending-sync, unmapped and mismatched fixtures produce their correct reconciliation states. A GL variance cannot be marked reconciled or silently adjusted away.
AC-16 Usability/security Oldest jobs appear first; all PDF rows are readable; spreadsheet formula-like text is safe; drill-down rechecks access; revoked/private links do not disclose report data.
AC-17 No side effects Running or retrying the report creates only reporting/delivery/audit records. It does not create customer invoices, change stock, approve prices, initialize tax rules or post GL adjustments.
AC-18 Performance On an agreed representative dataset, background generation meets approved generation/delivery and workshop-response budgets, with bounded query batching and visible failure handling. Numeric budgets must be set before release testing.

13. Suggested implementation sequence — not a commitment

  1. Confirm semantics and prerequisites: approve Section 12; verify real status, invoice, branch, advisor and sublet mappings; assess historical data quality.
  2. Build the read-only calculation and saved snapshot: verify counts, tax/charge parity, aging and exception handling with fixed fixtures.
  3. Add authorized report views and exports: enforce tenant, branch, recipient and financial rules on every output.
  4. Add scheduling and delivery: configure opt-in timing, shared snapshots, retries, auditing, recipient rechecks and failure alerts.
  5. Validate accounting and pilot: run approved GL comparisons and no-send tests; review pilot results before enabling live dispatch.

The requirements baseline is approved. This document does not record completed implementation or authorize live report dispatch. Confirm unresolved prerequisites before claiming full compliance; any reduced-scope release still requires explicit approval.
