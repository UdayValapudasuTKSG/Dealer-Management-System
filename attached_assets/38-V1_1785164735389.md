# Validation V1 — Coverage Completeness Matrix

This matrix maps every functional domain of AURA against its specification anchors (**see NC-2 reference map**) and records coverage status. Earlier drafts asserted that domains were covered *"unconditionally"*; that overstates the position. Coverage here means **application-logic + invariant coverage within the honesty scope of NC-12** — application correctness plus tenant/money/AI invariants — and is stated as `Full`, `Full (logic); NFR pending`, or `Partial + residual gap`. NFR readiness (DR/backup, encryption-at-rest, HA/uptime, tracing, message broker) is explicitly **not** claimed and is tracked as PENDING-INFRA. Residual functional gaps are enumerated in §V1.3 and classified **Pre-GA** (must land before general availability) or **Phase-2** (post-GA roadmap).

## V1.2 — Cross-Cutting Invariants Coverage

- **Tenant:** no cross-tenant read/write; foreign id → `404`; membership failure → `403` — covered (logic) per NC-1/NC-14.
- **Money:** immutable ledger, offsetting reversals, no gateway, USD-scale with GYD display conversion — covered (logic).
- **AI:** only A1/A6/A16 write autonomously and are audited + kill-switchable; deterministic actions are not "AI"; all advisory/HITL agents require human send/write — covered (logic) per NC-6.
- **Entitlement:** INV-ENT-1 server-enforced, un-entitled → `404` — covered (logic) per NC-9.
- **Honesty scope (NC-12):** r9 = defects in the BUILT app + corrected mandate (status: to-fix); V4 = residual open items of the spec/infra. NFRs are PENDING-INFRA and are the pre-GA blockers; this matrix does NOT claim production/NFR readiness.

## V1.4 — Interpretation Notes

- A `Full (logic)` status means the domain's application logic and its tenant/money/AI invariants are covered within the NC-12 honesty scope; it does **not** assert NFR/production readiness.
- Domains flagged *Partial — corrected-design target-state entity* are conformant at the spec level (r9 corrected mandate) but await schema promotion in the built app; they are tracked as Pre-GA to-fix items, not Phase-2 aspirations.
- Gaps in §V1.3 are the reason the word "unconditionally" is deliberately avoided: coverage is **conditional** on the corrected-design deltas landing and on PENDING-INFRA work being completed before GA.
