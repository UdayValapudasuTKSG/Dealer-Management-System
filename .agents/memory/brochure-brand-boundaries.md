---
name: Brochure brand boundaries
description: Dealer branding and oversized originals in the supplied BYD brochure collection.
---

The supplied BYD brochures are GT Automotive-branded. Do not automatically offer them under Beharry or another dealership merely because they sell the same vehicle models.

**Why:** Brochures contain dealer-specific pricing and contact details; cross-dealer reuse could send a customer to the wrong business.

**How to apply:** Inspect branding before extending brochure availability. Preserve uploaded originals; if an original is too large for email, use a clearly labelled, visually verified optimized derivative rather than silently disabling that brochure.

Brochure edits and archival affect future selections only; already queued customer emails must retain their selected attachment versions and filenames.

**Why:** Replacing a pricing brochure after a customer email was queued must not silently change the offer that staff selected.

**How to apply:** Keep queued attachment snapshots immutable when extending library management.

Finance offers are dealer-owned metadata, not values to infer from a model name across dealerships. The supplied model offers belong to the GT brochure set. A bank vehicle-loans webpage is a link, not an attached application PDF.

**Why:** Applying another dealership's finance figures or claiming a webpage is an attached form misrepresents the offer sent to the customer.

**How to apply:** Snapshot selected finance fields with the queued brochure email, preserve explicitly customized values, and require an actual PDF upload for models without a supplied brochure.
