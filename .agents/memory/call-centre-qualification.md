---
name: Call-centre qualification boundary
description: Why call-centre intake and sales handover are separate from the canonical sales phase machine.
---

Call-centre qualification is an intake ownership step, not completion of the existing sales qualification gates. “Transferred to Sales Advisor” is displayed while the sales phase is still new/contacted; subsequent sales progression uses the existing gates.

**Why:** A representative confirming interest authorizes a sales handover, not a quote, financing approval, or test drive. Adding automatic progression to qualified would bypass established prerequisites.

**How to apply:** Keep intake automation and stage-advance agents from progressing leads still waiting on a representative outcome. Preserve representative attribution after transfer for reporting, but do not treat historical attribution as permission to edit a sales advisor’s lead. Do not reassign historical owned enquiries when applying this new intake workflow.
