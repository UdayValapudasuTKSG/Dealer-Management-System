description: User-approved scope for estimate, pass-through cost and written-notice terms.
---
The user approved the three supplied terms only for quotations, estimates, proformas and pre-delivery sales invoices, not all AURA documents.

**Why:** These clauses concern price adjustments before delivery; adding them to final receipts, official forms or internal reports would be misleading. The user explicitly selected the narrower recommended scope.

**How to apply:** Preserve the supplied wording, including increases AND decreases passed through at cost without markup and written notice/revised invoice before delivery. Do not change pricing, tax or exchange-rate calculations as part of document wording. Leave receipts, completed-service invoices, official forms, reports and uploaded originals unchanged. Do not retain contradictory “price changes without notice” wording alongside these terms.
